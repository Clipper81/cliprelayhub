Privacy Policy - ClipRelayHub

ClipRelayHub accesses only the minimum TikTok account data required to publish video content on your behalf (your TikTok user ID and an access token). This tool does not sell, share, or use your data for advertising. Access tokens are stored securely on a private server and used solely to publish content you've approved. You can revoke access at any time via TikTok's app permissions settings, and you may request deletion of any stored data by contacting Chris@affiliatesimplified.co
